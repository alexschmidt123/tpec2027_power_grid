# Online computation time

See online_timing_statistics.csv and online_timing_statistics.json for mean and sample SD across evaluation seeds 1001--1003, in seconds per complete sequence. First average 512 episodes per training/evaluation cell, then average training seeds within each evaluation seed. This SD measures evaluation-seed variability of the averaged policies, not retraining or episode-level variability. Raw runs are unchanged; hardware metadata remain in source run_config.json.
